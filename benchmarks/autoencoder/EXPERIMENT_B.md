# Experiment B: oscillator system identification
Status: development pilot, not a final held-out study.

Question: Can a fixed oscillation representation support prediction of parameters of the generating system that are not directly measured by its construction?

## Simulation
Free underdamped response x(t)=A exp(-zeta*wn*t) cos(wn*sqrt(1-zeta^2)*t+phase)+baseline+noise. wn=2*pi*fn. Independently draw fn uniformly in [0.20,0.35] Hz, zeta in [0.005,0.04], initial amplitude in [1,2], phase in [0,2*pi], constant baseline in [-0.3,0.3], Gaussian noise standard deviation in [0.025,0.075]. All records are 20 seconds at 50 Hz. Train on 600 independent records; assess on 200 separate development-validation records. No final test data generated. Targets are simulator parameters zeta and fn, not measured period or amplitude. Mass, stiffness and damping coefficient are not separately identifiable without additional knowledge. Natural frequency is closely related to observed period in this weakly damped regime, so damping ratio is the stronger transfer test.

## Representations
Use existing data.objects unchanged, including smoothing, context and central 10-second selection. FG_mean_4 averages the original four measurements. FG_two_bins_8 averages those same measurements separately in each half of the central interval; this is an explicitly separate ordered aggregation, not a new oscillation measurement. It is motivated by preserving changes across cycles. Fit missing-value medians only on training. Report coverage. Raw, smoothed, spectrum and smoothed-spectrum baselines and MLP autoencoders have access to the same 20-second waveform; 4 and 8 latent dimensions, five seeds, 400 iterations for preliminary continuity with Experiment A. Same Ridge and Random Forest probes. Equation-aware nonlinear least squares on the raw waveform is a separate reference, not a generic representation baseline; the simulator family and frequency range inform it. All estimates are evaluated on validation data. Record loss curves, reconstruction metrics, coverage, per-record predictions, R2/MAE/RMSE and solver status.

## Limitations and next steps
The specified parameter ranges, noise levels, sample sizes and two-bin aggregation are proposed pilot decisions, not optimized or proven. Weak damping keeps observable cycles available but makes natural frequency nearly equivalent to inverse period. Mean amplitude alone can confound damping and initial amplitude; ordered measurements may help. A failed result is informative. The pilot trains autoencoders independently on this signal family: it tests reuse of fixed construction rules and downstream features, not zero-shot transfer of encoders trained in Experiment A. No claim of agent performance, cross-domain generalization or superiority to physics-based fitting. Select training configurations on development data, strengthen learned baselines (including CNNs), then freeze protocol and evaluate fresh test records.
